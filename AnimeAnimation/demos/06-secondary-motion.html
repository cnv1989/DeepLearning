Lesson 7 · Secondary motion: hair, ribbons, follow-through

What to notice

  • Move your mouse over the canvas, or turn on auto-sway. The head is the primary action. Twin tails, ribbons and the ahoge drag behind, overshoot and settle. That lag is secondary motion.
  • Stiffness controls how quickly a strand returns to its styled shape. Use high stiffness for short, gelled hair and low stiffness for long, silky hair.
  • Damping controls how quickly the wobble dies out. Low damping gives a bouncy, cartoony feel.
  • In Blender you get this from cloth or spring bones, in Live2D from its "Physics" settings, and in Spine from physics constraints. When you hand-draw, you offset the hair drawings by a frame or two behind the head.

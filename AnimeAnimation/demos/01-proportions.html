Lesson 2 · Proportions in heads

What to notice

  • The number of heads sets the age and tone of a character before you draw a single detail.
  • Chibi figures lose leg length first; tall figures gain it. The torso changes much less.
  • The white line marks the eye line, near the vertical middle of the head. In realistic drawing it sits a bit higher, so the face reads more adult.

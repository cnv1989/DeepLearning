Lesson 8 · Rigging and the walk cycle

What to notice

  • The rig is a bone hierarchy: hips → spine → neck → head, and hips → thigh → shin → foot. Rotating a thigh carries the shin and foot with it. That is forward kinematics (FK).
  • The four key poses of a step are contact, down, passing, up. The body is lowest on down and highest on up. Nothing animates the bob directly: it comes from planting the lowest foot on the ground.
  • Try the walk styles. Stride, lean, arm swing and knee lift alone give tired, sneaky and confident walks. Personality comes from the poses.
  • Anime walks are often 8 drawings on threes (one second per cycle) to save budget. Set "drawings per cycle" to 8 and "animate on" to threes.

Lesson 5 · Timing, spacing, and animating on twos

Experiments

  1. Switch Spacing to linear. The ball now moves at constant speed and looks robotic, as if it weighs nothing.
  2. Turn on onion skin and step frame by frame. With easing, the ghosts bunch up near the top of each arc (slow) and spread out near the ground (fast). That spacing is what gives the ball weight.
  3. Compare on ones / twos / threes. Twos is the anime default: 12 drawings a second, played at 24 fps. Threes looks choppier but costs a third of the drawings. Watch the drawing count in the bar under the canvas.
  4. Turn off squash & stretch. The impact loses its punch.

Lesson 6 · Limited animation: lip flap, blinks and holds

What to notice

  • Lip flap (kuchipaku) uses only three mouth drawings: closed, half and open. The audience accepts it as speech, and most TV anime dialogue is animated this way.
  • Vowel mode uses the five Japanese vowel shapes A / I / U / E / O. It's more convincing but takes more drawings.
  • Only the mouth and eyes move. The rest of the face is one held drawing. Holding most of the frame and animating a few small parts is the core budget trick of anime.
  • The blink uses 6 frames on ones: open, half, closed, closed, half, open. Fast blinks feel alive, and slow ones read as sleepy or deliberate.

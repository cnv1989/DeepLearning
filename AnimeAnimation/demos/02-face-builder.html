Lesson 3 · Face, eyes and expressions

Exercises

  1. Make the face look older using only three sliders. (Hint: eye size, eye line, chin.)
  2. Switch eye style between shoujo and shounen with every other setting the same. What changes in how the character reads?
  3. Click through the expressions. The eyebrows and mouth carry most of the emotion, even when the eyes barely change.
  4. Turn on construction to see the circle and jaw the head is built from. Draw these first when you sketch in Krita or Clip Studio.

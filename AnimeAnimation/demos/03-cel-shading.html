Lesson 4 · Line art and cel shading

What to notice

  • Left: smooth (realistic) shading. Middle: cel shading, the same lighting split into flat bands with a hard edge. Right: a 2D shape shaded the way you'd paint it by hand.
  • Drag on the canvas to move the light. The shadow edge moves with it, but it stays one clean shape.
  • Turn hue-shifted shadows off and the shadows go muddy and grey. Studio color designers choose a shadow color for each base color. The swatches below show the pairs.
  • The threshold slider is the "Color Ramp" position in a Blender toon shader. Raising it gives more shadow and a moodier look.

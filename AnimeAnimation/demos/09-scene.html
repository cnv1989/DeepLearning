Lesson 10 · Capstone: a full cut with an exposure sheet

How this cut is built

The whole shot is data. Each line in CUT below is a track, and the strip under the picture is a simplified exposure sheet (X-sheet / timesheet): one column per frame, one row per track. Every studio plans shots this way, and every program you'll use (Clip Studio, OpenToonz, Toon Boom, Blender) has a timeline that does the same thing.

  1. She looks off-screen (hold).
  2. Her head turns to camera, eased out.
  3. She says a line (vowel lip-sync, blinks on top).
  4. Surprise: a fast camera push-in with overshoot (easeOutBack), focus lines and a held reaction.

Your assignment

Open this file and edit CUT. Change the line, move the surprise earlier, swap the expression to angry, or add a tilt track. Then storyboard the same cut on paper and redraw it by hand in Krita or Clip Studio, using the X-sheet as your timing guide.

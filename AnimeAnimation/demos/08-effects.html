Lesson 9 · Action effects: smears, speed lines, impact frames

The shot, beat by beat

  1. Anticipation (frames 0–14): the orb pulls back and holds. The hold builds tension.
  2. Action (3–4 frames): it crosses the screen. Each frame is a smear, so the movement reads even with almost no in-betweens.
  3. Impact (2 frames): an inverted impact frame followed by a screen shake.
  4. Follow-through: focus lines that boil (re-drawn on threes with a new random seed), and sparkles.

Turn effects off one at a time to see what each contributes. Without them it's just a dot moving across the screen.
